What is the old republic era of star wars legends?

The old republic era covers the time period of 25,000 to 1,000 BBY. This stories are usually about the old republic or the jedi from the time period and the events that happened. Some examples are the sith wars, the mandalorian wars, the multiple galatic wars just to name a few.
